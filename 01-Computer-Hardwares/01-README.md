## ⚙️ How the Pieces Work Together

### Inside a Computer

When you open a program, its instructions and data are loaded from an **SSD or HDD** into **RAM**. The **CPU** executes instructions, while the **GPU** handles graphics and suitable parallel workloads.

The **motherboard** connects the components, and the **PSU** supplies electrical power.
